Keep the kiln lit

Kiln is free and open source, and stays that way. Sponsorship pays for build machines, code signing and new creator tools. Ko-fi and tips in Second Life are open now; GitHub Sponsors and Open Collective open with the first preview.

Four ways to give

GitHub Sponsors

Monthly or one-time, straight from your GitHub account. Best if you already follow Kiln there.

Ko-fi

A one-off tip by card or PayPal, no account needed.

Open Collective

Every expense is public, so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Tip in Second Life

Pay Linden dollars to kilnviewer Resident in-world, the way you would tip a builder: open the profile and choose Pay.
