Keep the kiln lit

Kiln is free and open source, and stays that way. Sponsorship will pay for build machines, code signing and new creator tools. Until it opens, a follow helps most.

Follow on GitHub

Four ways to give, opening with the first preview

GitHub Sponsors

Monthly or one-time, straight from your GitHub account. Best if you already follow Kiln there.

Ko-fi

A one-off tip by card or PayPal, no account needed.

Open Collective

Every expense is public, so you can see exactly where the money goes.

Tip in Second Life

Pay Linden dollars to the Kiln tip jar in-world, the way you would tip a builder.

Other ways to help

Report a bug

Something broken in Kiln? Tell us what happened, with your log if you can.

Suggest a tool

Describe what you are making and what slows you down.
